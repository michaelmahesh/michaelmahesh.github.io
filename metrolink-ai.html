Overview

Problem Statement

Commuters need clearer guidance on metro routes, interchanges, exits, and last-mile options.

Solution

Proposed Solution

A multilingual travel companion concept for Chennai metro users.

Users

Target Users

Chennai metro commuters and visitors.

Features

Key Features and Modules

  • Route and interchange guidance (planned)
  • Station and exit information (planned)
  • Multilingual support: Tamil, English, Hindi (planned)
