Research Evolution

From medical imaging to intelligent systems.

The research portfolio has developed across multiple application domains while maintaining a strong foundation in AI, deep learning and visual intelligence.

2018–2020

Medical Image Intelligence

Research involving brain tumour recognition, multimodal MRI analysis, segmentation and severity-level classification.

2021–2022

Video Analytics & Road Scene Understanding

Research expanded into surveillance-video anomaly detection, road scene segmentation and road intersection classification.

2023–2024

ADAS & Intelligent Transportation

Research included braking decision-making, pedestrian crossing-time estimation and intelligent transportation applications.

2025–2026

Multidisciplinary AI Systems

Recent work spans ADAS, medical AI, road anomaly detection, wireless intelligent systems and emerging AI architectures.
